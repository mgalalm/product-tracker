Product information
Product code: 701126

Warmer months can be hard work for your fridge freezer, but this Montpellier has a Super Switch that can help. It uses super freezing when the weather is warmer, so your frozen food will stay frozen, locking in the goodness. Now that's cool! There's a water dispenser in the fridge door so you can enjoy cool, crisp water any time – perfect after a workout or with dinner.

Good to know

- The fridge and freezer have a 50/50 split – stock up on both fresh and frozen food when you shop
- There's no metal elements inside which means low frost so you can spend less time defrosting

 Hassle-free installation

Door Reversal Service

Some freezers, fridge freezers and wine coolers can have their door hinged on either side.

Here's what we'll do:

Hinge the door on the opposite side to the factory setting.
Safety-check electric and plumbing connections (water & ice models).
Disconnect your existing appliance, unbox and check your new one.
Connect your new appliance to your existing connections.
Test the appliance and make sure there are no leaks and that it’s level.
Take the packaging away with us.
Please note: door reversal isn’t available for appliances delivered direct from the manufacturer.

Here's what you’ll need to do before we arrive:

Ensure necessary electric and plumbing connections are within 1.5 metres of where your appliance will go.
Check the dimensions of your appliance allow it to go where you want it.
Check the fridge freezer model you’re ordering has reversible doors (look in the specifications section of the relevant product page).
Note: we can’t install in garages or sheds.

 Remove and recycle your old appliance for FREE

To save you the hassle, when we deliver we can take away and recycle your old appliance, along with any packaging your new one comes in. Just add Let’s recycle on the Basket page.

Please note: You will need to disconnect your old appliance before we arrive, unless you’ve bought installation (excludes gas appliances - we cannot install these and they will need to be disconnected by a registered gas installer prior to our arrival). Please make sure your appliance has been emptied of any accessories you wish to keep, and is reasonably clean inside and out. In the case of fridges and freezers, we would prefer if these are fully defrosted before we arrive, but if this is not possible, our team will ensure it’s safe to transport by securing the doors tight to reduce any water leaking on our vans.
